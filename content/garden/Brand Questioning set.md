# Questions
> **Quick note:** You probably don't want to send all 35 to a customer… It'll get overwhelming quickly for them. Pick the ones you like the best for your project and reduce the list down to around 10 - 12

#### Brand Identity and Story
1. Tell us briefly about your business. What products or services do you provide?
   - Tell us about your current line of products and services: What specifically do you sell, and what is the primary problem or need that each offering solves for your customers? Based on that, where do you see the most exciting and realistic opportunities to expand your offerings or reach new markets in the near future?
1.  Which brands or organizations do you look up to and model or impact do you aspire to partner up with?
2. Why did you start your business, what was the motivator for it?
3. What mission statement and core values drive your brand? (if any exist today)
4. How do you ensure your brand is distinctly different and more valuable than others in the same field?
5. In 3 words, how would you characterize your brand's personality?
6. Besides personality traits, what are 5 words that capture your brand's essence (e.g., excellence, customer service, performance)?
7. What emotional triggers do you want your brand to evoke in potential customers (e.g. fear and precaution for a security company, "no-worry" organization for events firms)?
#### Target Audience and Market Position
7. Where would your brand fit into the customer's daily, weekly, or monthly life?
8. What regular struggles does your ideal customer encounter that you solve?
9. Can you share 3-4 examples of direct competitor websites?
10. What's your stance against those competitors? Is your field highly-competitive?
#### Visual identity and design preferences
16. Share 2-3 logo designs that inspire you and explain your preferences.
17. What color palette do you prefer for your brand, and are there any colors to avoid?
18. Do you have any specific typography or font styles in mind (e.g., serif, sans-serif, mono)?
19. How would you describe your brand's visual style (e.g., minimalist, sophisticated, playful)?
20. Are there specific design assets like images, fonts, illustrations that you've already produced or want to use?
#### Marketing and Promotional Strategy
21. What marketing materials do you anticipate needing (e.g., business cards, website)?
22. Describe your current or desired presence on social media and the most relevant platforms for your audience.
23. How do you intend to reach your ideal customer, and where do they typically spend time online? E.g. social, forums, whatsapp
24. What makes your brand’s offering unique against competitors?
#### Operational and Strategic Insights
25. What are your brand's short and long-term objectives? (e.g. short, make an impact with the new brand at X event; long, use the brand as leverage to close larger contracts)
26. Where do you envision your brand in 1, 3, 5 years? (e.g. a calm, steady-growth lifestyle business, a growth-driven tech startup)
27. What are your deadlines and timelines for this branding project?
28. How do you plan to implement the branding strategy in your business operations?
29. How do you prefer to provide feedback (e.g. email, messaging), and what turnaround time do you expect for revisions?
