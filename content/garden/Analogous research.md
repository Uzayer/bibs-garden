## what is analogous research

Analogous research is a design-research method that involves looking for solutions, insights, or inspiration in contexts outside of your usual area or industry. It asks the question, "Who else does what I do, but in a totally different way?" to find common challenges and approaches through different perspectives. This method encourages collaboration and empathy by connecting with people in unrelated fields or departments, allowing new and innovative ideas to emerge from these uncommon contexts. Often represented using tools like Venn diagrams or shared brainstorming sessions, analogous research helps break mental barriers and refresh problem-solving approaches by applying lessons learned elsewhere to your own challenges. It is widely used in design thinking and innovation processes to gain fresh perspectives and new lenses for problem-solving beyond the immediate environment or expertise.[maestrolearning +4](https://maestrolearning.com/blogs/analogous-research/)
Thus, analogous research is essentially about finding parallels and learning from how others solve similar problems in different environments to unlock creative and effective solutions for your own challenge.
